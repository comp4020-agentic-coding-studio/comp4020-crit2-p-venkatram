# Crit 2 reflection — Unsolicited redesign

The new site was built to ensure consistency with the existing brand and
aligns cohesively with the current Squarespace website. The chosen colour
theme consists of black, white, and various shades of purple. There were no
permission restrictions regarding the use of content or images from the
original Squarespace site.

## The breakthrough that moved the work forward

Based on research regarding website engagement and user retention, a suitable
template from Wix was adapted to complement Harmony's website, with specific
details integrated afterward. Having that wireframe to work from — rather than
starting from a blank page — was highly beneficial: it turned an open-ended
redesign into a series of concrete, specific changes against a known
structure.

## What this changed about the developer I want to be

Making small, specific changes across an existing website is harder than it
looks, and the project required significant verification and attention to
detail to get those changes right rather than assuming they'd landed. I also
learned that prompting is a difficult trial-and-error process when
experimenting with an unfamiliar approach, and that a strong understanding of
the underlying technical terms is what makes that iteration productive instead
of just guesswork. Practically, I found that if too many tokens are being used
without progress, switching to a more manual process is often the better call
— a lesson in knowing when to step back in and drive directly rather than
prompting around a problem.
