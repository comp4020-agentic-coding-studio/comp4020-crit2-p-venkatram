Who We Are

Every great dream begins with a dreamer

Founded in August 2008 by Ms. Sandra Oberoi, Harmony began with a vision: a robust music programme offering enriching and purposeful musical experiences for all.

Our vision

We are constantly renewing and strengthening the philosophical basis of music education and its visibility across India and the globe. Our teachers constantly ask questions and critically reflect on creating the most meaningful and impactful musical experiences for all learners, at all skill levels.

Nurturing curiosity

Classes are designed to nurture curiosity, creativity and skill-building in music, with students encouraged to recognise their strengths while teachers help them work through challenges toward musical excellence.

Beyond performance

Our aim goes beyond producing skilled performers — we teach value, tolerance and service through music, and to raise lifelong learners.

World-class network

We stay current with music education research through affiliations and global expert collaborations, delivering an unparalleled musical experience at par with leading programmes across the world.
