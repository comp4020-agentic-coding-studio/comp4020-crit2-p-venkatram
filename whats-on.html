Concert highlights

Our flagship charity concert series, Cadence, has run for over a decade — a small sample of the stages our students have performed on:

Harmony's 15th

An anniversary concert marking 15 years of musical brilliance.

UK Concert Tour '19

St Giles' Cathedral (Edinburgh), the UK Choir Festival in York, the University of York and The Nehru Centre, London.

Russia Concert Tour '18

Performances at The Cathedral of Christ the Saviour, Moscow, with Russian and Indian institutions.

Harmony for Healing Hands

A virtual concert (Nov 2020) with IIM Bangalore and IIM Allahabad, raising funds for Project Chaav and honouring healthcare workers.

Herbalife Concert

A 2018 fundraiser for The Herbalife Family Foundation, performed for an audience of over 19,000 people.

Cadence

Our annual flagship charity concert, showcasing student talent for a different cause each year.
